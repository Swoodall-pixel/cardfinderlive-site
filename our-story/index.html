Why I Built CardFinder Live

A collector's story.

How it Started

Hi, I'm Steven. Like a lot of collectors, I loved sports cards early on, drifted away for a while, and found my way back around 2020 when the hobby pulled me back in.

That brought me back to local card shows, regional events, and eventually The National. I really do love walking shows, digging through cases, and finding cards I wasn't even looking for. That's still one of the best parts of collecting for me.

The Moment Everything Clicked

At The National, I had a handful of cards I was hoping to find. I found a couple of them, but there were others I never came across.

What stayed with me afterward wasn't just that I missed those cards. It was realizing I had no way of knowing whether they had actually been there. That's when I started thinking: what if there was an app that made the cards at a show searchable before you spent hours wondering if they were even there?

CardFinder Live isn't here to replace the hunt. It's here to make the whole collecting experience better.

The Bigger Vision

The more I thought about it, the more I realized this wasn't just about finding one card at one show.

Whether you're sitting at home planning your weekend, waiting in line before a show opens, or walking the floor, the goal is the same: help collectors spend less time wondering what's out there and more time enjoying the hobby.

That's where things like discovering shows, preparing before you go, seeing what collectors are bringing, connecting beforehand, making cards discoverable, and coordinating real-world deals all started to fit together.

Building CardFinder Live

I realized this app should exist, but I had no idea how to build it. So I decided to learn.

It took me about eleven months to turn that idea into something real. CardFinder Live is still evolving, and that's the point.

A Note to Collectors

Thanks for taking the time to read my story.

I hope CardFinder Live helps you find that next great card.

Built by a collector. For collectors.
