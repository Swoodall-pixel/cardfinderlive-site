Common questions.

Is CardFinder Live free?

Yes. Downloading the app and using it is free.

How do deals work?

Find a card, message the collector in the app, agree on a public place, and meet in person to buy, sell, or trade. There's no buying inside the app — the deal happens face to face, like it should.

Do you take a cut?

No. No checkout, no fees, no payments through the app. The app just connects collectors; the deal is between you two.

Is my location tracked?

No. At a show, Go Live uses a quick check to confirm you're there — that's it. Nobody's exact location is ever shown to anyone.

Why I Built CardFinder Live

The idea for CardFinder Live started at the 2025 National. I found a few cards I was looking for, missed a few others, and started wondering: why isn't there an app that makes the cards at a show searchable? That simple idea became the starting point for CardFinder Live.
