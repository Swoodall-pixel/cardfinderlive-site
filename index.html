Why I Built CardFinder Live

Like a lot of collectors, I went to shows hoping to find a few specific cards. Sometimes I found them. Sometimes I didn't. What bothered me most wasn't missing a card—it was never knowing whether it had actually been there. That simple question eventually became CardFinder Live.
