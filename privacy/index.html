CardFinder Live Privacy Policy

CardFinder Live helps sports card collectors, dealers, and show attendees discover cards, card shows, card shops, and related app activity. This policy explains the information we collect, how we use it, and how users can contact us about privacy or data requests.

Information we collect

Depending on how you use the app, we may collect or process:

  • Account information, such as email address, username, user ID, and profile details.
  • Card listing information, including card details, photos, visibility settings, and related user-generated content.
  • Location information, including approximate or precise location when used for nearby cards, card shows, card shops, and related app functionality.
  • In-app messages and related message metadata.
  • App activity, such as searches, interactions, listing activity, saved items, and other app actions.
  • Device, notification, diagnostic, crash, and performance information.
  • Purchase status and entitlement information from Apple, Google Play, and RevenueCat, such as product IDs, purchase status, subscription/pass status, receipt or transaction identifiers, and access entitlement state. We do not collect raw credit card numbers or bank account details.
  • Support, safety, moderation, shop access, and account deletion requests that users choose to send us.

How we use information

We use information to:

  • Provide and maintain the app.
  • Create and manage user accounts.
  • Show cards, card shows, card shops, messages, and related app features.
  • Provide location-based discovery features when enabled by the user.
  • Process app access, purchases, subscriptions, passes, and restore-purchase state.
  • Send app notifications when enabled.
  • Respond to support, safety, moderation, and account/data requests.
  • Improve app reliability, diagnose crashes, and protect the service from abuse.

App Usage Analytics

CardFinder Live may collect information about how users interact with app features, including card discovery, Card Shows, saved items, messaging features, and card listings. We use this information to understand engagement, improve app functionality and reliability, and support internal business reporting.

Usage information may be associated with a user account for validation, security, and accurate measurement. We do not sell this information or use it to track users across other companies' apps or websites for advertising.

Service providers, including Firebase/Google, may process this information on our behalf. Our usage analytics do not include message contents or precise location histories in their event payloads.

We retain usage information only as necessary for these purposes, subject to applicable retention, deletion, and privacy requirements. Users may contact hello@cardfinderlive.com regarding their privacy rights and data requests.

Service providers

We use service providers to operate the app, including Firebase/Google services, Expo, RevenueCat, Apple App Store services, and Google Play services. These providers may process information as needed to provide authentication, hosting, database, storage, analytics, including Cloudflare Web Analytics for basic privacy-oriented website traffic and performance measurement, messaging, notification, purchase, entitlement, crash, diagnostic, and app distribution services.

Sharing

We do not sell personal information. We may share information with service providers that help operate the app, when required by law, to protect users or the service, or as directed by a user through app functionality.

User content

Card listings, card photos, profile details, messages, shop requests, and other user-generated content may be visible to other users depending on app features and user choices. Users should avoid posting private or sensitive information in public or shared areas of the app.

Account and data deletion

Users may request account deletion or data deletion by contacting support at hello@cardfinderlive.com or by using the support page at https://cardfinderlive.com/support/.

When we process a deletion request, we may delete or de-identify account information and user content where reasonably possible. Some information may be retained if needed for security, fraud prevention, legal compliance, dispute resolution, purchase records, tax/accounting records, backup integrity, or legitimate business operations.

Contact

For privacy questions, support, account deletion, or data deletion requests, contact hello@cardfinderlive.com.
