For help with CardFinder Live, account questions, card shop access, safety concerns, privacy questions, or data requests, contact support.

Email Support

Account deletion and data requests

Email support@cardfinderlive.com from the email address used for your CardFinder Live account. Include “Account deletion request” or “Data request” in the subject line.

Safety or abuse concerns

Email support with details about the issue. CardFinder Live may also include in-app safety, report, and block surfaces where available.

Card shops and operators

For card shop access, shop information, or operator questions, contact support from the email address associated with your account or business.

Real-world deals

CardFinder Live does not process per-card marketplace checkout in the app. Collectors message, plan, and meet in person to complete deals. Use public locations and personal judgment when coordinating meetups.
