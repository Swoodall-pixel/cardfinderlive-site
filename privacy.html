This Privacy Policy explains how CardFinder Live may collect, use, share, and protect information when you use the app. It may be updated as the product and its systems evolve.

1. Information we may collect

Depending on how you use CardFinder Live, we may collect account and app information such as your email address, authentication details, profile identity details, membership or access state, home state preference, app activity, messages, card shop access state, and alert or notification preferences.

2. Location and preference information

The app may use device location, home state preference, or related account settings to improve discovery and relevance. Location-related behavior may depend on your device permissions and your in-app settings.

3. Messages and activity data

If you use messaging or related dealmaking features, the app may store message content, thread activity, interaction timing, and related product-state information needed to support those flows and improve reliability.

4. Card shop access information

If you request, receive, or use approved card shop access, the app may store and use information related to that relationship, including request status, approved access state, and related account behavior.

5. How we use information

We may use information collected through CardFinder Live to operate and improve the app, support accounts and sign-in, personalize discovery and relevance, enable messages and related product features, support access, entitlement, and membership behavior, support alerts, notifications, and account awareness, protect users, shops, and the platform, and investigate abuse, fraud, safety issues, or misuse.

6. Notifications and alerts

The app may send or support in-app alerts, push notifications, or email updates depending on your account state, device permissions, app settings, and the features you use. Notification availability and delivery may vary by device, platform, permissions, or account state.

7. Paid features and transactions

If you use paid features, we may process and store information related to membership, passes, entitlements, transaction state, and account access. Billing and purchase handling may depend on Apple, the App Store, or other payment providers used for the applicable purchase flow.

8. Sharing of information

CardFinder Live does not sell personal information. We may share data with service providers, infrastructure providers, platform partners, payment providers, or other parties as needed to operate, secure, support, and improve the app, process purchases, investigate abuse or fraud, protect users, or comply with law.

9. Data retention

We may retain information for as long as reasonably necessary to operate the app, maintain account continuity, comply with legal obligations, resolve disputes, investigate misuse, and support product reliability.

10. Security

We take reasonable steps to protect information, but no system can be guaranteed perfectly secure. You are also responsible for maintaining the security of your account and device.

11. Your choices

Depending on the app surface and your account state, you may be able to review or change some profile details, location-related preferences, notification preferences, or account settings. You can also review device-level permissions through your operating system.

For account deletion or data requests, contact support@cardfinderlive.com from the email address used for your account.

12. Children’s privacy

CardFinder Live is not intended for children who are not legally able to use the service under applicable law. If we learn that information has been collected inappropriately, we may take steps to remove it.

13. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy over time as the product evolves. If we make material changes, we may update the revised version available in the app or through related product materials.

14. Contact

If you have privacy questions about CardFinder Live, contact us at support@cardfinderlive.com.
