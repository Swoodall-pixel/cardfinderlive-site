Shops and shows: you're already in.

Collectors use CardFinder Live to find cards, shows, and shops — 3,000+ shows and thousands of shops are listed in the app. If you run one of them, there's a free account waiting for you.

See yourself the way collectors do

Download the app and look up your shop or your show. That listing — your name, your address, your hours — is what a collector sees when they're deciding where to go this weekend.

If you run a card shop

Your listing is already live. We built it from public listings and went through them one by one to clean them up — but nobody knows your shop like you do.

Claim it, and two things happen. Your shop's card in the app flips to Verified, so collectors know the listing is really yours. And you can put your own cards in the app, as the store — a collector searching for a card finds yours, sees it's sitting in your shop, and comes in to get it. More traffic through your door is the whole idea.

Your cards do the inviting. A new collector finds one and comes in to get it — or a regular who wasn't stopping by today sees one he likes and makes the trip. And when two collectors make a deal in the app, it points them to a shop or a show in the middle to meet. Either way, people walk through your door.

Something off before you claim — wrong hours, old address, you moved? There's a button to tell us, and it comes straight to us.

Here's a real shop's page today

This is an actual shop page in the app right now — a real shop in New York. Look at the buttons: one tap and a collector can call the shop, get directions, or open its website. That's already live for every listed shop, and it's free.

Now look at the two things that are waiting. The page says "Not yet verified." And under "Inventory on CardFinder Live," the shelf is empty. Claim your shop — free — and both change: your page shows it's Verified with CardFinder Live, and the cards you tie to your shop fill that shelf, where collectors can find them.

If you host a card show

Collectors find your show two ways: the closest shows to home, and Plan a Trip when they're traveling — tell the app where you're going and it shows the shows there. Then the cards do the selling. A collector on his couch scrolls what's coming to your show, spots one he wants, messages the dealer, and makes a deal — now he's coming, when he wasn't before. One more person through your door, before your doors even open.

Show hosts get the same deal as shops: claim your free account in the app, and if your dates or details are wrong, the report button reaches us directly.

What it costs

Nothing. Being listed is free. The account is free. We built CardFinder Live so collectors can find you — that's the whole deal.

The plain fine print

Listing details come from public sources and submissions, and we re-check them. If we ever get something wrong, tell us through the app — or email support@cardfinderlive.com — and we'll fix it. A listing isn't an endorsement, and CardFinder Live doesn't sell cards or take payments — it just gets collectors to your door.
