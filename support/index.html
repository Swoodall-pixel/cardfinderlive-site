CardFinder Live Support

Need help with CardFinder Live? We're happy to help.

If you have questions about your account, purchases, or anything else, email us anytime at:

support@cardfinderlive.com

Account deletion

To permanently delete your CardFinder Live account and associated account data, send an email from the email address associated with your account.

Subject: Delete my CardFinder Live account

If available, include:

  • Your account email
  • Your username

Data deletion requests

If you'd like specific data removed without deleting your account, describe what you'd like deleted in your email.

Examples include:

  • Card listings
  • Card photos
  • Messages
  • Profile information
  • Location-related data
  • Shop access requests
  • Other account content

Information we may retain

Some information may be retained when required for:

  • Security
  • Fraud prevention
  • Legal compliance
  • Tax or accounting requirements
  • Backup integrity
  • Other legitimate business purposes

Still need help?

We can help with app access, purchases, restoring purchases, bug reports, safety reports, and general support.

If your question isn't covered above, just use the contact information at the top of this page and we'll be happy to help.
