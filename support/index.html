Card Finder Live Support

For help with Card Finder Live, contact support@cardfinderlive.com.

Account deletion

To request deletion of your Card Finder Live account and associated account data, email support@cardfinderlive.com from the email address associated with your account.

Please include: your account email, username if available, and the request “Delete my Card Finder Live account.”

Data deletion requests

To request deletion of specific data without deleting your full account, email support@cardfinderlive.com.

Please describe the data you want deleted, such as card listings, card photos, messages, profile information, location-related data, shop access requests, or other app content.

What may be retained

Some information may be retained if needed for security, fraud prevention, legal compliance, dispute resolution, purchase records, tax/accounting records, backup integrity, or legitimate business operations.

Other support

For app access, purchases, restore-purchase questions, safety reports, bug reports, or general support, email support@cardfinderlive.com.
